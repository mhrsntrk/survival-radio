  YES, need help
One up, one down
  (N): NO help
Mirror or screen:
  flash at it

  least 3 m, dark
  on light:
  V need help
  X need medic
  -> went this way

== RULE OF 3
You can last about
3 min without air
3 h in cold
  without shelter
3 days no water
3 weeks no food
Order: safety,
  shelter, water,
  signal, food

== WATER
Boil 1 min (3 min
  above 2000 m)
Or filter + tabs
Clear running
  water is better
Need 2-3 L a day,
  more in heat

== COLD, HEAT
Cold: off the
  ground, dry,
  wind shelter
Shivering stops,
  confused: warm
  the body core,
  sweet drinks if
  awake, no rub
Heat: shade, rest,
  water, cool the
  neck and armpits
Hot dry skin and
  confused: 112

== FIRST AID
Check danger first
Not breathing:
  112, then CPR:
  30 hard pushes,
  5-6 cm deep,
  100-120 a min,
  2 breaths
Bleeding: press
  hard, keep on;
  tourniquet only
  as a last resort
Breathing, out:
  on the side
  (recovery pos.)
Breaks: do not
  move, support
Keep them warm
  and still
